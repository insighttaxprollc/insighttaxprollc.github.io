Services
What we can help with

From a straightforward W-2 return to a complex small business — every engagement handled with the same precision and personal attention.

Individual tax — Form 1040

W-2 filers, self-employed (Schedule C), rental income, K-1s, capital gains, multi-state, and foreign income including FBAR and Form 8938.

Small business tax

S-Corporation (1120-S), Partnership (1065), and C-Corporation (1120) returns, owner K-1s, state filings, and new-entity setup guidance.

Bookkeeping & cleanup

Monthly QuickBooks Online bookkeeping, reconciliation, and financial statements. Catch-up and cleanup for behind or disorganized books.

Payroll & sales tax

Payroll processing, quarterly 941 filings, year-end W-2s and 1099s, and state sales & use tax filings on a monthly or quarterly schedule.

IRS & state notices

Notice review and plain-language explanation, written response preparation, and representation under Power of Attorney (Form 2848).

Tax planning & advisory

Quarterly estimated tax strategy, year-end planning, and ongoing advisory so tax season has no surprises. Entity formation and setup guidance.
